Nicska

pronounced “NEES-ka”

The IPSC shot timer you already carry.

It beeps, listens to every shot and shows the first shot, splits and total time. Free, open source and offline.

What it does

Random delay and beep

Standby, a random delay and a saturated 2700 Hz beep, loud enough for the range. Optional “Standing by” voice.

Hears the shots

The app recognizes its own beep and measures everything from it, so sound latency cancels out, even with a Bluetooth speaker.

Splits, total and scoring

First shot, splits, total time, hit factor and ranking. Review the timeline, delete or add shots.

Video with your times

Record the attempt on video (up to 4K) and export an MP4 with the timer and shots drawn on top.

History and charts

Drills, sessions, progress and day-to-day comparison. A JSON backup that you keep yourself.

For everyone

Start by tapping the phone, 3/2/1 vibration for deaf shooters, themes and high contrast, Portuguese, English and Spanish.

Real privacy

No account, no ads, no trackers. The app does not even request the internet permission: your times, audio and video stay on your device and leave only when you share them. Read the privacy policy.

Independent project, not affiliated with IPSC, CBTP or timer makers. Not an official match timer: under IPSC rule 9.10.1 only a Range Officer's timer gives the official time. Use it to train.
